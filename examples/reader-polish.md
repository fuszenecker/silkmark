# Reader polish

This document demonstrates the v0.37 reading layout.

## Comfortable rhythm

Long-form Markdown benefits from whitespace between paragraphs and sections. SilkMark now gives headings more breathing room while keeping body text compact.

> Block quotes are visually distinct without becoming heavy.
> They remain selectable and support inline **formatting**.
